Reliable testing depends on reliable operations. Alongside verification activity, I helped develop and deploy practical automation that reduces repetitive work and gives teams a clearer view of machine and test status.

## Focus

- Real-time interaction with test equipment to keep a machine's logical baseline aligned with the relevant system controller.
- Version-control-aware workflows for tracking machine status and controlled updates.
- Python-based internal test automation to support repeatable execution and data handling.
- Earlier Python tooling for automated log capture and software flashing in vehicle system testing.

## Impact

At Technology & Strategy, process analysis and automation improved operational efficiency by **50% within six months**. Understanding the complete workflow made it possible to remove time loss, absorb increased demand, and scale the team without sacrificing quality.

> Details of Avilus internal tools and interfaces are deliberately withheld. The emphasis here is on the engineering approach: controlled updates, traceability, repeatability, and useful automation.
