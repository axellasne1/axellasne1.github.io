My interest in aircraft began in an aeromodelling club at age 12, where I built and flew model aircraft. It grew into private-pilot training and a practical appreciation of how technical decisions affect operators.

## Experience

- Private Pilot Licence holder with approximately **113 hours** of dual and solo flight.
- Long-standing model-aircraft builder and pilot.
- DJI Mavic 2 Pro pilot, using aerial imagery to document travel and environments.
- Simulator-based remote-control operation of company aircraft for test purposes.

## Why it matters in verification

Flying does not replace disciplined engineering, but it makes test results tangible. It reinforces the importance of predictable system behaviour, clear operating boundaries, and evidence that supports a confident decision before a pilot is involved.

I am particularly interested in roles that connect HIL and systems verification to flight-test preparation and execution.
