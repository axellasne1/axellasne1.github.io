## Abstract

This article demonstrates the Distill layout in the portfolio and highlights how verification thinking supports technical decisions before release.

## Motivation

A reliable engineering program depends on evidence, realistic validation, and disciplined review before a system is entrusted to operation.

## Method

1. Define the operational context.
2. Validate the edge conditions that matter.
3. Check evidence quality before release.
4. Communicate residual risk clearly.

## Result

The value is not only in finding defects early, but in making the release decision transparent and defensible.
